# Canopy 0.2.0

An original MIT-licensed shader pack for Minecraft Metal, inspired by public Vibrant Visuals screenshots and documentation. Not an official Mojang product, port, or reproduction of its renderer. Requires Minecraft Shader Loader 0.1.3 and Minecraft Metal 0.2.2; Solstice remains a separate choice.

BALANCED enables pixel-aligned short-range shadows, foliage transmission, material-dependent highlights, biome atmosphere, native block clouds, bounded screen-space water reflections, depth-colored transmission, procedural pixel caustics, and restrained bloom. FAST removes screen-space reflection and reduces shadow cost. Scene resolution stays native.

Material presets and explicit pixel masks in `shaders/materials.json` generate a packed material atlas at resource loading. The first library covers 50 sprites, including mixed-material ore and lamp masks. Those masks are annotations of Minecraft 26.3 vanilla texture layouts and are guarded by the corresponding albedo file hashes. No vanilla color textures, Mojang Vibrant Visuals MERS textures, code, or reference screenshots are shipped. Resource packs can supply their own material maps; uniform materials support animated sprites, while varying animated masks are skipped pending a synchronized animation contract. This is not a complete PBR resource pack. Water reflections are limited to visible opaque geometry; offscreen content falls back to the sky. Caustics and leaf scattering are artistic approximations. Fog is distance/height/direction based, without volumetric shadow-ray marching. Biome parameters follow the camera with smoothing. Colored light propagation and dynamic local-light shadows are not implemented.

Build: `./gradlew :shader-loader:canopyPack`. Put `Canopy-0.2.0.zip` in the game profile's `shaderpacks` directory. Set `pack=Canopy-0.2.0.zip` and `profile=BALANCED` in `config/minecraft-shader-loader.properties`. Use Shader Loader 0.1.3 with Minecraft Metal 0.2.2. See `docs/canopy.md` and `docs/materials.md` in the repository for source analysis, material authoring, testing and performance.
